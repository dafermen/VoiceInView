# vReader Privacy Policy — publication draft
Last updated: 2026-10-07.

vReader creates English captions using your iPhone microphone and on-device speech recognition. Microphone access is requested when you choose to listen. Audio is processed in memory and is not saved or uploaded by vReader.

Final captions and session details may be saved locally on your iPhone. Auto-save is on by default and can be disabled before a session. You can open, rename, export or delete sessions. Local sessions are excluded from device backup. Deleting the app removes its local sessions; export anything you want to keep.

An initial English speech model installation may require an Internet connection and uses Apple's system model installation services. Installed compatible models allow captioning to be used without Internet. Model/device readiness and actual offline behavior should be tested before a conference.

Exporting, copying or sharing is your choice. The destination application or service may transmit or sync the transcript according to its own terms and privacy policy. Copies you export are not removed when you delete a session from vReader.

vReader has no required account, advertising, tracking or analytics. App preferences are stored locally. Local storage capacity is checked to display readiness and prevent write failures; the capacity value is not transmitted.

Provisional captions may change or be incomplete. Speech recognition accuracy varies with device, environment and speakers.
